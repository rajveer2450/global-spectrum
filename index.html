From the pit to the plot

Global Spectrum mines and supplies coal, limestone and sand, hauls them by road, and deals in land and property. Based in Maihar, Madhya Pradesh.

We mine it

We take on contract mining for lease-holders, and supply coal, limestone and sand to cement plants, power plants and builders.

We work across Maihar, Satna, Rewa, Sidhi, Singrauli, Katni and Jabalpur, close to the cement plants of the Maihar and Satna belt and the Singrauli coalfield.

What we supply

  • CoalFor power plants and industry
  • LimestoneFor cement plants
  • River sandFor builders and contractors
  • M-sandManufactured sand for concrete and plaster

Looking for something that isn’t listed? Ask us.

Then we move it

Our own trucks, plus attached trucks when an order needs more, carry it from the mine or stockyard to your plant or site.

Own and attached trucks
We run our own fleet and bring in attached trucks for large orders, so one call covers the whole job.
Where we deliver
Maihar, Satna, Rewa, Sidhi, Singrauli, Katni and Jabalpur, and further on request.
Paperwork
Each load travels with its weighbridge slip and the transport papers the law requires.

How many loads is that?

200 truckloads by road

And we deal in land

We buy, sell and lease land and buildings, from plant and stockyard sites to plots and homes in town.

  • Industrial landSites for plants, crushers, stockyards and sidings, with land-use conversion handled.
  • PlotsResidential and commercial plots across the districts we work in.
  • BuildingsOffices, warehouses, sheds and homes to buy or rent.
  • LeasingLand and sheds on yearly leases for miners, transporters and traders.
  • Title and registryRecord search, mutation and registration, start to finish.
